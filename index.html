MELODY BARS CREATIVE GUIDE

Give your idea a sound.

A strong music prompt usually combines these building blocks:

01Genre & influence

Name a genre or blend two styles in an original way.

02Energy & emotion

Describe the mood, pace and feeling you want listeners to remember.

03Sound palette

Call out instruments, vocal texture, percussion and production details.

04Song journey

Ask for a strong intro, intimate verse, rising pre-chorus and memorable hook.
